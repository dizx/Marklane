# A little room to think.

Welcome to **Marklane**. A quiet place for your notes, ideas, and everything taking shape.

Start writing in Markdown. Move to another line and the formatting falls into place. Click a paragraph to see its syntax again.

## Make yourself at home

- **Write naturally.** Headings, lists, and emphasis appear as you go.
- *Stay in the flow.* Press Ctrl+Shift+F for a distraction-free canvas.
- Keep the original. Ctrl+/ switches to Markdown source.
- Find your way. Use the outline on the left to jump between sections.

## Small steps, good ideas

- [x] Find a little space to write
- [ ] Put the first thought on the page
- [ ] See where it takes you

> You don't need the whole story to write the first line.

## A few useful details

| To do this | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| Save your work | Ctrl+S |
| Make it bold | Ctrl+B |
| Find a phrase | Ctrl+F |
| Open a document | Ctrl+O |

Inline `code` has its own space. So do bigger ideas:

